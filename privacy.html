Last Updated September 8, 2026

Your privacy matters. This page explains what information may be collected when you visit Alone at the Altar and how that information may be used.

1. Introduction

Alone at the Altar is the official website for the true story written by Bradley Hobbs. The website provides information about the book, its story, its chapters, and related content.

This Privacy Policy explains the types of information that may be collected when you visit this website and how that information may be used.

Alone at the Altar and Bradley Hobbs are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third party websites.

7. Information Security

Reasonable efforts are made to maintain the security of this website. However, no method of transmitting information over the internet or storing electronic data can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

This website is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate consent.

If you believe that personal information belonging to a child has been submitted through this website, please use the Contact page to request assistance.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time as the website changes or additional services are added.

Updates will be reflected on this page with a revised Last Updated date.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please visit the Contact page.
