About the Book

Some Things
Cannot Be Held
Together Alone.

Alone at the Altar is a deeply personal story about faith in the middle of circumstances that could not be controlled.

It is about the prayers spoken when no one else knew what was happening. It is about fatherhood, love, loss, and the painful realization that there are some things in life that one person cannot hold together alone.

Through seasons of questions and uncertainty, the altar became a place where there was finally room to stop pretending, stop trying to have every answer, and bring everything honestly before God.

From Bradley Hobbs

I Did Not Write
This Story Because
I Had Every Answer.

I wrote it because there were moments when I did not know what to do with the questions I carried.

I wrote it because there were prayers I prayed that nobody else heard.

And I wrote it because I eventually learned that God had heard every one of them.
