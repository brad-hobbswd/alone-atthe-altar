More Than a Biography

I Did Not Begin
Writing Because
Life Was Easy.

I write because there are moments in life that cannot always be explained while you are living through them.

Some experiences leave questions behind. Others leave memories that continue to speak long after the moment has passed. Writing became one way of giving those moments a place to be honest.

Alone at the Altar comes from that place. It is not a story written from a distance. It was shaped by faith, fatherhood, loss, prayer, and the continuing discovery that God remains present even when life becomes difficult to understand.

01 The Story Behind the Writing

Before I Knew
What to Say,
I Had Songs.

There were seasons when I did not know what to pray, who to talk to, or how to explain what was happening inside me. I kept working. I kept showing up. I kept going to church. And I kept praying.

But sometimes I did not have the words.

Special Love and Hold Me While I Cry became more than songs to me. They became reminders of God's presence when I could not see a way forward. When I did not know what else to do, I could listen, pray, and remember that God was still there.

Those songs became part of the thread that kept me close to God through a season I did not understand. I would listen. I would pray. Sometimes I would cry. Sometimes I would simply sit quietly before God.

I did not know then how much those moments would become part of my story.

Eventually, that season became years. Six and a half years of marriage, family, responsibility, questions, separation, loss, and trying to understand what I could not control.

And through it all, I kept finding my way back to God.

That is where Alone at the Altar was born. Not from having all the answers, but from discovering that even when I felt completely alone, God had never left me alone.

I was alone at the altar, but I was never alone with God.

Why I Write

Because Some Things
Need to Be Said
Honestly.

I am not interested in pretending that faith means life will never hurt.

Faith has carried me through beautiful moments, but it has also remained with me through questions, uncertainty, disappointment, and seasons when I did not know what I was supposed to do next.

My writing comes from the belief that honesty does not weaken faith. Sometimes honesty is the place where faith finally has room to breathe.
